## Questions to think through

1. **Name.** Change specifications, Change requests, or Requirements specifications?
2. **Where it sits.** These are low-level, post-design specifications before development, not the high-level requests that come from customers. Do both need a place, and how do they connect?
3. **Types.** Functional (from a use case) vs. non-functional (bounded context / refactoring). Do we classify them at all?
4. **IDC / RDC.** How does an impact analysis turn into a chain of independently (or reliably) deployable changes, e.g. add the new field, move the dependents, then remove the old one? What does an agent receive: the spec, or the deployment chain?
5. **What stays from the current design:** saved selection, impact by shared dependency, decisions to answer, review per revision, share, publish to Jira / ClickUp / Notion.
